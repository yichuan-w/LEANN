# Frequently Asked Questions

## Before the Trip

**Q: Do I need a passport?**
A: Yes. A valid passport is required for all guests. Your passport must not expire within 6 months of the travel date (September 22, 2026 or later). If you don't have a passport, apply immediately — processing takes 6-8 weeks (expedited: 2-3 weeks for an additional $60).

**Q: Do I need a visa for any of the ports?**
A: No. U.S. passport holders do not need a visa for Mexico, Cayman Islands, or Jamaica for stays under 30 days.

**Q: Is travel insurance required?**
A: Not required, but strongly recommended. Our partner offers comprehensive coverage for $89/person covering medical emergencies, trip cancellation, and lost luggage. Contact us to enroll.

**Q: Can I bring my kids?**
A: Absolutely! Kids ages 3-12 have access to the complimentary Kids' Club. Children under 3 must be supervised by a parent at all times. Cribs are available upon request (limited supply — request at booking).

**Q: What's the dress code?**
A: Casual during the day (swimwear at pool areas only). Smart casual for dinner (no tank tops, flip-flops, or swimwear in the dining room). Formal attire required for Captain's Dinner on Night 4 (Day 4) — suits/dresses recommended.

**Q: Can I bring my own alcohol?**
A: No. All alcohol will be confiscated at security check-in. You may purchase a drink package onboard for $69/person/day (unlimited beer, wine, cocktails, and non-alcoholic drinks) or buy drinks individually.

## Onboard

**Q: Is Wi-Fi available?**
A: Yes. Two packages: Basic ($12.99/day — email and messaging) and Premium ($19.99/day — streaming and video calls). Purchase at Guest Services or pre-purchase on our website for 15% off.

**Q: How do I pay for things onboard?**
A: Everything is charged to your cabin key card, which is linked to a credit card at check-in. Cash is not accepted onboard. You'll receive an itemized bill on Day 7.

**Q: Are there medical facilities onboard?**
A: Yes. The ship has a fully equipped medical center with a doctor and nurse on duty 24/7. Note: medical visits are not free — charges start at $150 for a consultation and are not typically covered by regular health insurance. This is why we recommend travel insurance.

**Q: What if I have dietary restrictions?**
A: The ship accommodates vegetarian, vegan, gluten-free, kosher, and halal diets. Please notify us at least 14 days before departure so the kitchen can prepare. Inform your waiter on the first night as well.

**Q: What happens if I miss the ship at a port?**
A: It is your responsibility to return to the ship before the posted "all aboard" time. The ship will NOT wait. If you miss the ship, you must arrange your own transportation to the next port at your own expense. Travel insurance may cover this.

## Shore Excursions

**Q: Do I have to book shore excursions?**
A: No. Excursions are completely optional. You can explore ports on your own, stay on the ship, or book an excursion. We recommend booking through us for guaranteed return-to-ship timing.

**Q: Can I book excursions onboard?**
A: Yes, subject to availability. However, popular excursions (Dunn's River Falls, Stingray City) often sell out. Pre-booking is recommended.

**Q: Is it safe to explore ports on my own?**
A: Generally yes, but we recommend staying in tourist areas, keeping valuables secure, and returning to the ship at least 1 hour before departure time. Grand Cayman and Cozumel are very tourist-friendly. In Montego Bay, stick to the Hip Strip area or book a guided tour.

## Health & Safety

**Q: What COVID/health protocols are in place?**
A: As of January 2026, no COVID test or vaccination proof is required. However, if you feel ill before or during the cruise, please contact the medical center immediately. Hand sanitizer stations are available throughout the ship.

**Q: Is there a gym onboard?**
A: Yes. The fitness center on Deck 12 is open 6:00 AM – 10:00 PM daily. Equipment includes treadmills, weights, spin bikes, and yoga mats. Group fitness classes are complimentary.

**Q: Are there laundry facilities?**
A: Self-service laundry rooms are available on Decks 6, 8, and 10 ($3/wash, $3/dry). Dry cleaning service is available through your cabin steward (priced per item).

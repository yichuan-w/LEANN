# Caribbean Dream Cruise — March 15-22, 2026

## Trip Overview

Welcome aboard the **Caribbean Dream Cruise**, departing from Miami, Florida. This 7-night cruise visits Cozumel (Mexico), Grand Cayman (Cayman Islands), and Montego Bay (Jamaica) aboard the Royal Voyager vessel.

### Departure Details

- **Departure Date:** Saturday, March 15, 2026
- **Departure Port:** Port of Miami, Terminal J
- **Check-in Time:** 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM (boarding closes at 3:30 PM sharp)
- **Vessel:** Royal Voyager
- **Cabin assignments:** Sent via email 5 days before departure

### Return Details

- **Return Date:** Saturday, March 22, 2026
- **Estimated Arrival:** 7:00 AM at Port of Miami
- **Disembarkation:** 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM (by deck number, announced onboard)

### What to Bring

- Valid passport (must not expire within 6 months of travel)
- Printed or digital boarding pass
- Credit card for onboard purchases
- Sunscreen, swimwear, comfortable walking shoes
- Formal attire for Captain's Dinner (Night 4)
- Any prescription medications in original containers

### What NOT to Bring

- Alcohol (will be confiscated at security)
- Drones
- Irons or steamers
- Surge protectors with built-in USB ports are allowed; power strips are not

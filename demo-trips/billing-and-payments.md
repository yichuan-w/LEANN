# Billing & Payment Information

## Package Pricing

| Cabin Type | Per Person (Double Occupancy) | Single Supplement |
|---|---|---|
| Interior | $899 | +$450 |
| Ocean View | $1,199 | +$600 |
| Balcony | $1,499 | +$750 |
| Suite | $2,299 | +$1,150 |

**Group discount (10+ guests):** 15% off cabin price
**Group discount (20+ guests):** 20% off cabin price + 1 free cabin (interior)

## What's Included in the Package

- 7-night accommodation
- All meals in the Main Dining Room and Buffet
- Room service (standard menu, 24/7)
- Entertainment shows and onboard activities
- Use of pools, gym, and fitness center
- Kids' club (ages 3-12)
- Port fees and taxes

## What's NOT Included

- Shore excursions (booked separately, prices listed in itinerary)
- Specialty dining restaurants ($35-$65 per person)
- Alcoholic beverages (drink package available: $69/person/day)
- Spa treatments
- Wi-Fi (packages: $12.99/day basic, $19.99/day premium)
- Gratuities ($16/person/day auto-charged, can be adjusted at Guest Services)

## Payment Schedule

- **Deposit:** $250/person due at booking (non-refundable after 60 days)
- **Second payment:** 50% of remaining balance due by January 15, 2026
- **Final payment:** Full balance due by February 15, 2026
- **Late payments:** $50 late fee applies after due dates

## Accepted Payment Methods

- Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover
- Bank wire transfer (contact accounting@flywithpeggs.com)
- Payment plans available: 3 monthly installments (no interest if paid by final due date)

## Refund & Cancellation Policy

- **90+ days before departure:** Full refund minus $50 processing fee
- **60-89 days:** 75% refund
- **30-59 days:** 50% refund
- **15-29 days:** 25% refund
- **14 days or less:** No refund
- **Travel insurance:** Strongly recommended — covers medical emergencies, trip interruption, and lost luggage. Available through our partner for $89/person.

## Onboard Spending

- All onboard purchases charged to your cabin key card
- Link a credit card at check-in (required)
- Daily spending limit: $300/day default (can be increased at Guest Services)
- Itemized bill delivered to cabin on Day 7 — review and dispute at Guest Services before disembarkation

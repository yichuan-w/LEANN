# Daily Itinerary — Caribbean Dream Cruise

## Day 1 — Saturday, March 15: Miami Departure

- 11:00 AM – 2:00 PM: Check-in at Terminal J, Port of Miami
- 3:30 PM: All aboard (boarding closes)
- 4:00 PM: Mandatory safety drill (muster station assigned on your cabin key card)
- 5:00 PM: Ship departs Miami
- 6:30 PM: Welcome dinner (Main Dining Room, Deck 5)
- 8:00 PM: Welcome show at the Grand Theater (Deck 7)

## Day 2 — Sunday, March 16: Day at Sea

- Full day at sea – enjoy pools, spa, casino, and onboard activities
- 9:00 AM: Morning yoga on the Sun Deck (complimentary)
- 12:00 PM: Poolside BBQ lunch
- 3:00 PM: Trivia competition (Atrium Lounge, Deck 4)
- 7:00 PM: Dinner
- 9:30 PM: Comedy show

## Day 3 — Monday, March 17: Cozumel, Mexico

- 8:00 AM: Arrive at Cozumel
- Shore excursions available:
  - Snorkeling at Chankanaab Park ($65/person, departs 9:00 AM, returns 1:00 PM)
  - Mayan Ruins of Tulum ($89/person, departs 8:30 AM, returns 3:00 PM)
  - Beach Break at Paradise Beach ($35/person, shuttle every 30 min)
  - Downtown Cozumel walking tour (complimentary, departs 9:30 AM)
- All guests must be back onboard by 5:30 PM
- 6:00 PM: Ship departs Cozumel

## Day 4 — Tuesday, March 18: Grand Cayman

- 7:30 AM: Arrive at George Town (tender port — boat shuttles to shore)
- Tender boats run every 15 minutes from 8:00 AM to 4:00 PM
- Shore excursions available:
  - Stingray City ($75/person, departs 8:30 AM)
  - Seven Mile Beach day pass ($45/person)
  - Rum Point catamaran tour ($95/person, includes lunch)
  - Turtle Centre & Hell tour ($55/person, departs 9:00 AM)
- All guests must be back onboard by 5:00 PM
- 5:30 PM: Ship departs Grand Cayman
- 7:30 PM: Captain's Dinner — formal dress code (Main Dining Room)

## Day 5 — Wednesday, March 19: Day at Sea

- Relax day — pools and spa open 7 AM to 10 PM
- 10:00 AM: Cooking class (Deck 9, sign up at Guest Services, $25/person)
- 2:00 PM: Art auction (Atrium)
- 4:00 PM: Belly flop competition (Pool Deck)
- 7:00 PM: Dinner
- 9:00 PM: Live music in the Jazz Lounge

## Day 6 — Thursday, March 20: Montego Bay, Jamaica

- 9:00 AM: Arrive at Montego Bay
- Shore excursions available:
  - Dunn's River Falls ($79/person, full day, departs 9:30 AM)
  - Bob Marley Museum tour ($55/person, departs 10:00 AM)
  - Bamboo rafting on the Martha Brae River ($70/person, departs 10:00 AM)
  - Hip Strip shopping and beach ($30/person, shuttle available)
- All guests must be back onboard by 6:00 PM
- 6:30 PM: Ship departs Montego Bay
- 8:00 PM: Jamaican-themed dinner night

## Day 7 — Friday, March 21: Day at Sea

- Final full day at sea
- 11:00 AM: Farewell brunch (Buffet, Deck 9)
- 2:00 PM: Luggage talk in the Atrium (important disembarkation instructions)
- Set luggage outside cabin door by 10:00 PM (except carry-off items)
- 7:00 PM: Farewell dinner
- 9:30 PM: Farewell show

## Day 8 — Saturday, March 22: Return to Miami

- 7:00 AM: Arrive at Port of Miami
- 8:30 AM – 10:30 AM: Disembarkation by color-coded groups (announced Day 7)
- Express Walk-Off: Available for guests carrying their own luggage, starts at 7:30 AM

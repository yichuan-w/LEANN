# Transportation & Pickup Information

## Getting to Port of Miami — Terminal J

### Group Shuttle (Included for Groups of 15+)

- **Pickup locations and times:**
  - **Miami International Airport (MIA):** 9:00 AM from Arrivals Level, Door 2 (look for "Caribbean Dream" sign)
  - **Fort Lauderdale Airport (FLL):** 8:00 AM from Ground Transportation, Bus Bay 4
  - **Hilton Miami Downtown:** 10:00 AM from hotel lobby
  - **Marriott Biscayne Bay:** 10:15 AM from hotel lobby
- Shuttle is a 56-seat charter bus with luggage storage
- Travel time: approximately 20-45 minutes depending on pickup location
- **You MUST confirm your shuttle spot by March 10, 2026** — email transport@flywithpeggs.com

### Driving / Self-Transport

- **Port address:** 1015 North America Way, Miami, FL 33132, Terminal J
- **Parking:** $22/day at the port garage (pre-pay at portmiami.biz for $20/day)
- **Uber/Lyft:** Drop-off at Terminal J passenger drop-off zone

### Flights — Recommended Arrival

- If flying in the day of departure, arrive no later than 10:00 AM to allow time for:
  - Baggage claim (~30 min)
  - Shuttle or taxi to port (~30 min)
  - Check-in and security (~45 min)
- **Recommended:** Fly in the night before and stay at a partner hotel:
  - Hilton Miami Downtown: Group rate $149/night (code: CARIBDREAM26)
  - Marriott Biscayne Bay: Group rate $139/night (code: PEGGSTRAVEL)

## Return Transportation

- **Return shuttle:** Departs from Terminal J at 10:00 AM and 11:00 AM
- Drop-off at Miami Airport (MIA) only — approximately 25 minutes
- If flying out on return day, book flights departing after 1:00 PM to be safe
- **Recommended:** Book flights departing after 2:00 PM if checking luggage

## Luggage

- **Allowed:** 2 checked bags + 1 carry-on per person
- **Label all luggage** with the provided cruise tags (mailed 2 weeks before)
- **Checked bags:** Leave outside cabin door by 10:00 PM on Day 7
- **Carry-on:** Keep essentials for Day 1 (medication, swimsuit, change of clothes) as checked luggage may not arrive to your cabin until evening
